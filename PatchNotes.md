## Sun Ce and Sun Quan

Sun Ce's provincial spirits carry over to Sun Quan:

- **Triumphal Renown:** Gain 2% Prestige for each war won as lead attacker, up to 30%.
- **Naval Tradition:** Each coastal core grants 0.5% War Power, up to 50%.
- **Coastal Commerce:** Each coastal core grants 1% Trade Profit, up to 50%.

Sun Ce pawns the Imperial Seal to recover his father's veterans, only for Yuan Shu to use it to proclaim himself emperor. Establish five coastal cores to bring Zhou Yu, Jiang Qin, Zhou Tai, and Zhang Zhao into Sun Ce's service. After the duel at Shenting, Taishi Ci asks leave to gather his scattered soldiers and promises to return by noon. Do you take him at his word? From 200 onward, Sun Quan can succeed his brother as governor. Secure Danyang, Wu, and Kuaiji to recruit Lu Meng, Lu Xun, Ding Feng, and Xu Sheng. When Cao Cao marches south, decide whether to stand with Liu Bei at Red Cliffs. Later, Lu Meng offers to seize Jing with soldiers disguised as boatmen, at the cost of that alliance.

## Kong Rong

Kong Rong's provincial spirits reward scholarship, diplomacy, and peace:

- **Confucian Scholarship:** Each Confucian core tile reduces Research Cost by 1%, up to a 50% reduction.
- **Expanded Diplomacy:** Gain one additional Diplomat.
- **Peaceful Renown:** While at peace, gain 1% Prestige per point of positive Stability, up to 25%.

Kong Rong talked his way into Li Ying's hall at the age of ten. Now he keeps his own hall open to scholars in Beihai, including Mi Heng, whose wit makes him as risky to recommend to the Han court as he is hard to ignore. A victory recalls Beihai's debt to Taishi Ci, who rode through the Yellow Turban siege to fetch Liu Bei: send thanks to Liu Bei or gifts to the rider. When Cao Cao marches against Liu Bei, decide whether to stand by your public rebuke. Annex and core all of Xu to accept 1,000 gold from Donghai's merchants or restore Xiapi's farms for three years of higher land tax and tile output. Make Qufu a core to restore the Temple of Confucius for prestige or reopen its schools for administrative points. A foothold in Liaodong lets you invite exiled scholars home or recruit local horsemen.
