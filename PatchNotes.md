I'm happy to announce that Patch 0.33 is now available on Steam (beta). This patch adds two more Three Kingdoms warlords.

## Sun Ce and Sun Quan (Wu)

Sun Ce laid the foundations of Wu, one of the Three Kingdoms, which Sun Quan later led to the imperial throne. Together, they feature 20 events drawn from the novel, tracing Wu's rise from conquering Jiangdong to proclaiming an empire. Sun Ce's events focus on establishing a foothold in Jiangdong and unifying the southeast. A special succession event then passes the torch to Sun Quan, whose events center on turning Wu into a major power in China.

Their Provincial Spirits reward aggressive expansion and coastal dominance:

- **Triumphal Renown:** Gain 2% Prestige for each war won as lead attacker, up to 30%.
- **Naval Tradition:** Each coastal core tile grants 0.5% War Power, up to 50%.
- **Coastal Commerce:** Each coastal core tile grants 1% Trade Profit, up to 50%.

Sun Ce pawns the Imperial Seal to recover his father's veterans, only for Yuan Shu to use it to proclaim himself emperor. Establish five coastal core tiles to bring Zhou Yu, Jiang Qin, Zhou Tai, and Zhang Zhao into his service. After the duel at Shenting, Taishi Ci asks leave to gather his scattered soldiers and promises to return by noon. Do you take him at his word?

From 200 onward, Sun Quan can succeed his brother as governor. From 202, secure Danyang, Wu, and Kuaiji to recruit Lu Meng, Lu Xun, Ding Feng, and Xu Sheng. When Cao Cao marches south, decide whether to stand with Liu Bei at Red Cliffs. Later, Lu Meng offers a plan to strike at Jing with soldiers disguised as boatmen, at the cost of peace with Liu Bei.

## Kong Rong

Kong Rong comes with 10 events, and his Provincial Spirits reward scholarship, diplomacy, and peace:

- **Confucian Scholarship:** Each Confucian core tile reduces Research Cost by 1%, up to 50%.
- **Expanded Diplomacy:** Gain one additional Diplomat.
- **Peaceful Renown:** While at peace, gain 1% Prestige per point of positive Stability, up to 25%.

Kong Rong talked his way into Li Ying's hall at the age of ten, and now keeps his own hall in Beihai open to scholars. A victory recalls Beihai's debt to Taishi Ci, who rode through the Yellow Turban siege to bring Liu Bei's relief force: send thanks to Liu Bei or gifts to the rider. Later, choose whether to recommend the brilliant, sharp-tongued Mi Heng to the Han court or keep him at your own. When Cao Cao marches against Liu Bei, decide whether to stand by your public rebuke.

Beyond Beihai, annex and core all of Xu to either accept 1,000 gold from Donghai's merchants or restore Xiapi's farms for three years of higher Land Tax and Tile Output. Core Qufu to restore the Temple of Confucius for Prestige, or reopen its schools for Administrative Points. A foothold in Liaodong lets you invite exiled scholars home or recruit local horsemen.
