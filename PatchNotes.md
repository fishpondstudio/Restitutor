I am pleased to present Patch 0.16. As we are approaching early access, I am keeping the patch smaller and more frequent to avoid dropping a giant patch before release and break everything.

[h2]Capital Relocation[/h2]

A new [b]Relocate Capital[/b] action lets you move the provincial capital to another core tile of your province:

[list]
[*]Relocation requires the target tile to be your core and at peace, costs 1 Capital Relocation Point, and cannot be performed again for 24 months.
[*]Moving the court unsettles the province: -10 Stability for 24 months.
[*][b]Capital Relocation Point[/b] has been added as a possible Restoration Bonus. It can also be acquired via events and missions.
[/list]

[h2]New Missions and Achievements[/h2]

Two new missions are available to any province:

[list]
[*][b]A Province Transformed[/b] - Your province now commands twice the territory it once held, and new cities and peoples look to the governor's seat for order. Plan a new provincial capital, secure allies in the Senate, or empower the provincial government.
[*][b]Every Art Mastered[/b] - Research every technology available to the Roman world, from agriculture and law to engineering and war. Found a capital of learning, give scholars a voice in the Senate, or dispatch experts across the province. Unlocks the achievement The School of Athens.
[/list]

I have also reworked Lugdunensis' missions - they are not longer restricted by date, which is more consistent with other provinces. Lugdunensis also receives five new events expanding its mid-game campaign:

[list]
[*][b]Masters of the Rhine Frontier[/b]
[*][b]The Southern Gateway[/b]
[*][b]The Road to Britannia[/b]
[*][b]A Coast Bound Together[/b]
[*][b]The Integration of Aquitania[/b]
[/list]

Belgica's missions has also been reworked: Northward into Germania and Across the Sea to Britannia now demand four tiles each (up from two) and are no longer restricted by date.
