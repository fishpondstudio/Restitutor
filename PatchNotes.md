I am pleased to present Patch 0.11, arriving on schedule as we continue toward Early Access. This update introduces Historical Narratives spanning 400 A.D. to 476 A.D., a new Barbarian Raid mechanic, and unique provincial content for Aquitania, Belgica, and Narbonensis, including new spirits, missions, and events. All three provinces are now playable.

[h2]Historical Narratives & Barbarian Raids (400 A.D. - 476 A.D.)[/h2]

During this time period, the Western Empire was entering its final age: frontiers buckled under the pressure of migrating peoples, armies struggled to defend an immense territory, and imperial authority steadily slipped from Rome's grasp. Across the Rhine, the Danube, and even within the Empire itself, new powers arose to claim the lands Rome could no longer hold.

This patch brings that turbulent era to life through a new series of historical narratives and the [b]Barbarian Raids[/b] mechanic. Between 400 and 476 A.D., historical events can establish the Suebi, Visigoths, Vandals, Burgundians, Huns, Franks, Saxons, Alemanni, and Ostrogoths. These peoples seize tiles from existing provinces and found polities of their own, reshaping the political map as the West fragments.

For their first 20 years, newly formed barbarian polities receive bonus War Power and can launch a raid each year. Raids are special wars that last for one year, reducing the target province's Land Tax, Tile Output, and Stability for their duration. A targeted province can end a raid immediately by paying a ransom in Gold or spending Infiltration to subvert it. Other provinces can also intervene: inciting a raid makes it more devastating, while deterring one reduces the raider's War Power.

Once those first 20 years have passed, the polity loses its special War Power bonus and will no longer launch raids. From then on, it plays by the same rules as any other province.

The narrative culminates in 476 A.D. with the fall of the Western Roman Empire. The event [b]An Empire Ends in Silence[/b] unlocks the choice to pursue [b]Our Own Destiny[/b], granting a lump sum of Consul Points at the cost of permanently withdrawing from Senate elections.

[h2]Aquitania: Provincial Spirits, Missions, Events[/h2]

Stretching from the Atlantic coast to the Pyrenees, Aquitania was a land of fertile river valleys, prosperous vineyards, and wealthy rural estates. Burdigala stood at the heart of its commerce and learning, while the province's hills and mountain approaches formed a natural bastion in southwestern Gaul.

Aquitania receives three new Provincial Spirits inspired by this landscape and history. [b]Serene Vineyards[/b] rewards a stable realm, [b]Cultivated Estates[/b] turns development into greater tax income, and [b]Hillfort Bastion[/b] makes the province's rugged core a source of defensive strength.

[list]
[*][b]Serene Vineyards[/b] - +1% Tile Output for each positive Stability.
[*][b]Cultivated Estates[/b] - +1% Land Tax for each Tile Upgrade.
[*][b]Hillfort Bastion[/b] - +1% Defense to all tiles for each core Hill tile.
[/list]

Aquitania's new mission chain begins with an alliance across the Loire, then turns south toward a Mediterranean port and the conquest of Narbonensis. From there, an ambitious ruler can cross the Pyrenees and begin a wider expansion into Iberia.

[list]
[*][b]A Gallic Alliance[/b] - Ally with Lugdunensis when relations are favorable.
[*][b]A Port on the Mediterranean[/b] - Annex Narbonensis tiles and secure a Mediterranean harbor at Agatha for trade or fortification.
[*][b]The Submission of Narbonensis[/b] - Reduce Narbonensis to a rump state and make it a client.
[*][b]Across the Pyrenees[/b] - Expand into Tarraconensis, gaining War Power or Stability and Casus Belli against the Iberian provinces.
[/list]

[h2]Belgica: Provincial Spirits, Missions, Events[/h2]

Belgica guarded the northeastern approaches to Gaul, from the Channel coast to the Rhine frontier. Its roads, granaries, and fortified cities sustained Rome's northern armies, while Augusta Treverorum grew into an imperial residence. It was a wealthy borderland, but one perpetually shaped by military pressure from beyond the Rhine.

Belgica's three new Provincial Spirits reflect its martial traditions and frontier role. [b]Ranged Predominance[/b] rewards armies built around ranged troops, [b]Bravest of the Gauls[/b] develops victorious generals, and [b]Martial Society[/b] converts greater conscription into greater War Power.

[list]
[*][b]Ranged Predominance[/b] - +1% War Power for each 1% of Ranged composition.
[*][b]Bravest of the Gauls[/b] - +1 General Skill Point after winning a war.
[*][b]Martial Society[/b] - +1% War Power for each 1% of Actual Conscription.
[/list]

Belgica's missions embrace an outward-looking military strategy. Drive south into Lugdunensis, advance across the Rhine into Germania, establish a route across the Channel to Britannia, and ultimately prove that Belgica has become a great power in its own right.

[list]
[*][b]Southward into Lugdunensis[/b] - Press south along the Great Roads with Casus Belli against Lugdunensis.
[*][b]Northward into Germania[/b] - Carry standards into Germania or fortify the Rhine frontier.
[*][b]Across the Sea to Britannia[/b] - Prepare the Channel crossing for an invasion of Britannia.
[*][b]Belgica Ascendant[/b] (requires 20 core tiles, 10,000 War Power, and 10 victories) - Unlocks a choice of endgame rewards: General Skill Points or Diplomatic and Administrative Points.
[/list]

[h2]Narbonensis: Provincial Spirits, Missions, Events[/h2]

Narbonensis linked Italia, Iberia, and the rest of Gaul along the Mediterranean coast. Ancient ports such as Narbo and Massilia, together with the Via Domitia and the Rhône corridor, made it one of the West's great commercial crossroads and among the most deeply Romanized regions beyond Italia.

Narbonensis gains three Provincial Spirits drawn from its privileged cities, busy harbors, and far-reaching commercial ties. [b]Municipal Privilege[/b] expands construction in core territories, [b]Maritime Prosperity[/b] enriches harbor trade, and [b]Commercial Alliances[/b] turns diplomacy into additional Trade Capacity.

[list]
[*][b]Municipal Privilege[/b] - Core tiles get +1 Building Slot.
[*][b]Maritime Prosperity[/b] - Harbour gives +10% Trade Profit.
[*][b]Commercial Alliances[/b] - Each diplomatic treaty gives +1 Trade Capacity.
[/list]

Narbonensis's missions make use of its position at the crossroads of the Western Empire. Cultivate alliances with Italia and Aquitania, invest the wealth of Narbo, draw Corsica into your sphere through marriage and diplomacy, and negotiate an Alpine exchange to secure a more favorable frontier.

[list]
[*][b]The Fruits of Italian Friendship[/b] - Ally with Italia to gain a choice of Administrative, Diplomatic, or Military Points.
[*][b]The Fruits of Aquitanian Friendship[/b] - Ally with Aquitania to gain Tile Output, Land Tax, or War Power.
[*][b]The Golden Coffers of Narbo[/b] (requires 300 monthly revenue and 10,000 Gold) - Finance merchants for additional Trade Capacity and Trade Profit, or invest in estates for greater Land Tax.
[*][b]The Corsican Marriage Settlement[/b] (requires 5,000 Gold, a marriage, and a treaty with Corsica) - Pay a 5,000 Gold dowry to make Corsica a client.
[*][b]An Alpine Exchange[/b] (requires alliance with Italia, specific Alpine tiles cored) - Trade Alpine tiles with Italia for mutual benefit or refuse with Prestige gain.
[/list]
