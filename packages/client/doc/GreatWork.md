# Great Works

Great Works occupy specific named tiles and provide a unique mechanic or modifier. All selected tiles exist in `Rome.json`, and every Great Work was completed before 700 CE.

| Name | Tile | Completed | Image | Suggested mechanic |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |

| Temple of Artemis | Ephesus | c. 323 BCE | [Image](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3APhilip_Galle%2C_after_Maerten_van_Heemskerck%2C_Dianae_Ephesiae_Templum_%28The_Temple_of_Diana_at_Ephesus%29%2C_1572%2C_NGA_156112.jpg) | **Sanctuary of Artemis:** visiting pilgrims and merchants generate income; the city recovers more quickly from unrest. |

## Placement notes

- `Rome.json` has no Giza tile. The Great Pyramid uses Memphis as the regional tile for the Memphite necropolis; it is the only Great Work not placed on its exact settlement.
- Great Works completed after the campaign begins in 192 CE can appear through dated construction or activation events rather than existing at game start.
